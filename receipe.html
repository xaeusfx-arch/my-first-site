Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Butter or margarine, softened
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 3/4 cup Brown sugar, packed
  • 2 Eggs, large
  • 2 teaspoons Vanilla
  • 2 1/4 cups Flour, unsifted
  • 1 teaspoon Baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon Salt
  • 1 cup Walnuts or pecans, chopped (optional)
  • 2 cups JCC Bittersweet 60% Cacao Baking Chips

Baking Instructions

  1. Preheat oven: Heat your oven to 375ºF (or 350°F depending on the batch style).
  2. Cream butter and sugars: In a large mixing bowl, beat the softened butter with the white sugar and brown sugar at medium speed until creamy and lightened in color.
  3. Add wet ingredients: Add the eggs and vanilla extract one at a time, mixing on low speed until incorporated.
  4. Combine: Gradually blend the dry mixture into the creamed mixture. Stir in the chocolate chips and optional nuts.
  5. Shape: Drop the dough by tablespoons (or spoonfuls) onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
  6. Bake: Bake for about 9 to 11 minutes (or around 10 minutes at 350°F) until the edges are golden brown or nicely browned.
  7. Cool and enjoy: Let them cool slightly before serving.
